About

Afonso Rodrigues is a Portuguese journalist in Lisbon who works with data and with a camera.

Investigations

The investigations start from public data: INE, Pordata, Eurostat, municipal registers and freedom-of-information requests. Most begin with a plain question, such as why rent is rising faster in one neighbourhood than the next, or who is being pushed out and where to. Then they follow the figures until there is an answer, or a better question.

Photography

The photographs are documentary work from Lisbon, Istanbul, Coimbra and Thailand, made over repeated visits to the same streets. New series are added when they are finished.

Independent work.
